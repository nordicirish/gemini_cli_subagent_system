Volume · rVol
Shares traded today. rVol compares that to a normal day at this time: above 1.5x is unusual interest, above 2x is strong.
ATR %
Typical daily price swing. Higher means wider moves, so stops need more room.
RSI
Momentum gauge from 0 to 100: above 70 is stretched (red), below 30 is oversold (green).
VWAP
Average price paid today, weighted by volume. Price above it (▲) means buyers are in control; below (▼) means sellers are.
MACD · Trend
Momentum direction (Bull / Bear) and the short-term price trend (Up / Down / Flat).
Target
The next Fibonacci price level and how far away it is. "Cap" is the most the stock is expected to stretch today.
Dealer (GEX)
Options dealers' gamma exposure. Positive: their hedging tends to calm swings. Negative: it tends to amplify them. V+ / V− is vanna flow from the options volatility surface.
Signal (−6 to +6)
GEM's combined setup score with a plain-English label. "Overheated" means a strong trend that is stretched (RSI 75+) — avoid chasing.
